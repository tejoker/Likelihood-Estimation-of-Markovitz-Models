Rejection rates at $\alpha=0.05$ with Wilson 95\% intervals. Every replication uses the windows and the test stream of the same replication of E2 (families a--d, independent windows) or E2-dep (marginal law fixed exactly); only the critic network differs. MLP: the default W-critic (3 hidden layers of width 128); pooled: two Conv1d blocks (64 channels, kernel 5), mean over time, dense head. MMD and KS: the same replications of E2 / E2-dep. McNemar: exact two-sided test on the discordant replications. $^{\dagger}$ KS assumes i.i.d.\ returns and is not valid within GARCH windows. Mean time per test (one thread): pooled 66 s, MLP 9 s.
