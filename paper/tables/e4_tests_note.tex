Rejection shares at the 5\% level across the 30 universes (W-critic: Driscoll-Kraay HAC p-value, 10-block time split; MMD: wild-bootstrap p-value with a fixed bandwidth per universe), for the first synthetic seed and for twice the median p-value over 2 seeds. Bracketed intervals are Wilson 95\% intervals that assume independent universes; universes share every TEST date and overlap in tickers, so the effective number of independent comparisons is smaller and the shares are descriptive. Placebo size: rejection rate of the same test when both samples are independent draws from the fitted generator (H0 true; 60 replications per generator cycling over universes; Wilson 95\% interval). Size-adjusted: rejection when the p-value is below the 5\% quantile of the placebo p-values of the same generator and test (Monte Carlo p-value against the placebo, pooled over universes). The reference row compares real TEST with the last 615 TRAIN days (same number of windows); it has no placebo. Mean stat.: mean over universes and seeds of the critic statistic $T$ or of MMD$^2_u$.
