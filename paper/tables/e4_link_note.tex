Spearman correlations between test quantities and utility losses (``e4\_link'': replica-wise portfolios, the DESIGN measure, which includes the estimation-noise term; ``e4\_link\_pooled'': portfolios from the moments of the pooled replicas, which isolate the covariance misspecification). Test quantities are seed means; the studentised statistics replace the p-values, which lose resolution for strong rejections. Pooled: over the 150 (universe, generator) points. Within-universe: across the 5 generators of each universe, averaged; the permutation $p$ permutes generator labels within universes and is conditional on these generators. Within-generator: across the 30 universes of each generator, averaged; the permutation $p$ permutes universes within generators; it is insensitive to generator-level offsets of a statistic (such as the placebo bias of the critic). Brackets: universe-bootstrap 95\% percentile intervals (universes share dates, so they are optimistic). Generator-level: Spearman of the 5 generator means, exact two-sided permutation $p$ over the 120 orderings; this is the only view whose sampling unit is the generator, so the evidence across generators rests on n = 5, and conclusions are conditional on these generators.
